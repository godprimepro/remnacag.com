Credit

REMNAC INVESTMENTS AG provides flexible and tailored financing solutions to established businesses across a broad range of industries. Our credit strategy includes private credit, structured financing, and strategic capital solutions designed to support business expansion, liquidity requirements, acquisitions, and long-term growth objectives. We work closely with business owners, management teams, and established companies seeking adaptable financing structures aligned with their operational and strategic priorities. Our approach emphasizes disciplined capital allocation, long-term partnerships, and customized financing solutions across both private and public market opportunities. Through our Direct Partnership Solutions platform, we provide tailored financing opportunities for founder-led and independently operated businesses seeking strategic long-term capital support and financial flexibility.

REMNAC INVESTMENTS AG Overview

REMNAC INVESTMENTS AG’s financing capabilities complement the firm’s broader investment activities, enabling us to provide flexible capital and financing solutions tailored to the evolving needs of established businesses. Our approach allows us to support companies through a range of strategic financing structures designed to align with long-term operational and growth objectives.

REMNAC Strategic Credit

REMNAC Strategic Credit is designed to provide investors with access to diversified private credit and structured financing opportunities across a range of industries and market segments. The strategy primarily focuses on senior secured financing structures positioned higher within the capital structure, with an emphasis on capital preservation, disciplined underwriting, and long-term risk management during varying market conditions.
